Xboost

Find useful conversations on X and prepare replies that you review and send yourself.

How to use Xboost

  1. Choose or create a profile. A profile holds one product, its X account, keywords and AI context.
  2. Open an X search. Xboost scores posts as they appear and highlights useful conversations.
  3. Prepare a draft. Select a post for optional Codex drafting, then review and edit the result.
  4. Reply yourself. Check the signed-in X account before you fill and send the reply.

Xboost does not automatically publish, like, follow or switch your X account.

Choose a profile

Selecting a profile opens a relevant X search. You can switch again from the toolbar at any time.

Scoring stays local. Optional AI drafting sends selected posts to your LAN server and OpenAI. Xboost never publishes, likes or follows for you.
